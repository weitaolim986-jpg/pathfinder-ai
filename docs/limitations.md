# Known Limitations and Responsible Use

## Retrieval Consistency

A relevant direct link may exist in the Knowledge Hub but not be retrieved for every response. The fallback is an exact search phrase, but this is less convenient for users.

## Financial Guidance

Financial-aid eligibility depends on citizenship, household income, programme, current policy, and application timing. The agent provides planning guidance, not a guaranteed aid decision.

## Career Outcomes

Career and salary outcomes vary by:

- individual ability
- internships
- technical and communication skills
- industry conditions
- location
- further education

The agent must not guarantee employment or salary.

## Decision Scorecards

Scores are heuristic thinking tools. They are not psychometric tests, admissions predictions, or measures of a student’s potential.

## Overseas Study

Visa, work, and immigration rules change. Users must verify current rules using official government sources.

## Application Support

The agent can help with brainstorming, structure, and draft review. Students remain responsible for truthful, original submissions.

## Scope

The current Knowledge Hub has stronger coverage for selected NUS programmes and initial UK/Australia overseas resources. Coverage should not be interpreted as a complete university database.
