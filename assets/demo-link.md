# PathFinder AI Demo

## Scenario Demonstrated

A prospective NUS student compares Business Analytics and Data Science while considering affordability and financial safety.

## Capabilities Shown

- student-context summary
- structured programme comparison
- financial-risk framing
- decision scorecard
- financial-aid guidance
- official programme and aid resources
- backup pathways
- actionable next steps

## Privacy Note

The demonstration uses a synthetic student scenario and does not expose private user information.
